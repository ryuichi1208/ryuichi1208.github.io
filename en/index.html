I support service reliability from both hands-on engineering and community activities. My experience spans the full stack — from Linux kernel-level file system development, to operating infrastructure serving ~600 million monthly page views, to SRE practice at GMO Pepabo and IVRy. My strengths are the practical knowledge of keeping systems running — incident response and EOL handling — and spreading SRE culture through organizing SRE NEXT (2025 Co-Chair), writing, and speaking. I currently work as an Senior SRE at IVRy, Inc., building the observability platform and driving reliability improvements for a conversational voice AI SaaS. I co-authored "The Knowledge Map of SRE" (Gijutsu-Hyohron).
